COMMERCIAL LEASE DEED

This Lease Deed is made on this 15th day of April 2026 at Coimbatore between M/s Kongu Estates, a partnership firm represented by its managing partner Mr. P. Senthil, having its office at 21, Avinashi Road, Coimbatore 641018 (the "Lessor"), and M/s Anbu Medicals, a proprietorship represented by its proprietor Mr. A. Anbalagan, having its address at 4, Race Course Road, Coimbatore 641018 (the "Lessee").

1. DEMISED PREMISES
The Lessor hereby leases to the Lessee the ground floor shop measuring 450 square feet at 21, Avinashi Road, Coimbatore 641018 for running a pharmacy.

2. LEASE PERIOD
The lease shall be for 36 months from 1 May 2026, with a lock-in period of 12 months applicable to both parties.

3. RENT
The monthly rent shall be Rs. 60,000/- exclusive of GST, payable on or before the 7th of every month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Lessee has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 3,60,000/-, which shall be refunded within 30 days of the Lessee handing over vacant possession of the premises, after adjusting any unpaid rent.

5. RENT ESCALATION
The rent shall be increased by 10% after every 12 months of the lease period.

6. MAINTENANCE
The Lessee shall carry out minor repairs at its cost. Structural repairs, property tax and the common area maintenance of the building shall be borne by the Lessor.

7. TERMINATION
After the lock-in period, either party may terminate this lease by giving 3 months' prior written notice to the other party.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute shall first be attempted to be settled amicably, failing which it shall be referred to arbitration at Coimbatore under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

In witness whereof the parties have set their hands on the day and year first above written.
