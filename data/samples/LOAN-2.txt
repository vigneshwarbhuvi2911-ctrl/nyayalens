HOME LOAN AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is made on 12/01/2026 at Bengaluru between Nilgiri Housing Finance Limited, a housing finance company registered with the National Housing Bank, having its office at 45, Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025 (the "Lender") and Mrs. Meena Krishnan, residing at Flat 3B, Lotus Apartments, Jayanagar 4th Block, Bengaluru 560011 (the "Borrower").

1. THE FACILITY
The Lender shall make available to the Borrower a home loan of Rs. 45 lakh for the purchase of the residential flat described in the Schedule (the "Property").

2. COST OF CREDIT
The facility carries a floating rate of interest, currently 8.9% per annum, linked to the Lender's external benchmark rate. Any change in the benchmark rate shall be passed on to the Borrower with effect from the next reset date, with prior intimation by SMS and email.

3. REPAYMENT SCHEDULE
The loan shall be repaid in 240 monthly instalments of Rs. 40,199/- each, payable on the 10th day of every month, commencing from 10th February, 2026.

4. PART-PAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE
As the loan carries a floating rate of interest, the Borrower may part-pay or foreclose the loan at any time without any prepayment charge.

5. DELAYED PAYMENT
Any instalment not paid by its due date shall attract additional interest at 2% per annum on the overdue instalment for the period of delay.

6. SECURITY
The loan shall be secured by an equitable mortgage by deposit of title deeds of the Property in favour of the Lender, which shall be released within 30 days of full repayment.

7. DEFAULT
If the Borrower fails to pay three consecutive instalments, the Lender may recall the loan after giving the Borrower 60 days' written notice.

8. DISPUTES
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction over all disputes arising out of it.

9. NOTICES
Notices shall be sent to the addresses given above, or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

Signed by the Lender and the Borrower on the date mentioned above.
