CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-COMPETE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 15 July 2026 between Nimbus Retail Private Limited, having its office at 77, Linking Road, Mumbai 400050 (the "Company"), and Ms. Farah Sheikh, an independent consultant residing at 14, Hill Road, Bandra, Mumbai 400050, email farah.sheikh@example.com (the "Consultant").

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
The Consultant shall keep confidential all information relating to the Company's customers, suppliers, pricing, sales data and business plans that the Consultant receives during the engagement.

2. DURATION OF CONFIDENTIALITY
The Consultant's obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement shall continue in perpetuity and shall survive the termination of the engagement indefinitely.

3. NON-COMPETE
For a period of 24 months after the end of the engagement, the Consultant shall not work for, advise or provide services to any business that competes with the Company anywhere in India.

4. INDEMNITY
The Consultant shall indemnify and keep the Company indemnified against all losses, damages, costs and expenses of whatever nature suffered by the Company arising from any breach of this Agreement by the Consultant.

5. INJUNCTIVE RELIEF
The Consultant agrees that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Company, and that the Company shall be entitled to seek an injunction in addition to any other remedy.

6. RETURN OF MATERIALS
On the end of the engagement, the Consultant shall return all documents and data belonging to the Company.

Agreed and signed by the Company and the Consultant.
