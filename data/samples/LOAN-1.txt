This Personal Loan Agreement is made and executed at Chennai on this 5th day of March, 2026,

Kaveri Finance Limited, a non-banking financial company having its registered office at 14, Greams Road, Chennai 600006 (hereinafter referred to as the "Lender"), of the ONE PART;

AND

Mr. Arun Prakash, son of Mr. K. Prakash, residing at 22, Lake View Road, West Mambalam, Chennai 600033, mobile 9840012345 (hereinafter referred to as the "Borrower"), of the OTHER PART.

1. DEFINITIONS
In this Agreement, "Loan" means the amount sanctioned under Clause 2, "EMI" means the equated monthly instalment payable under Clause 4, and "Due Date" means the 5th day of every calendar month.

2. LOAN AMOUNT AND DISBURSEMENT
The Lender agrees to lend and the Borrower agrees to borrow a sum of Rs. 5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakh only). The Loan shall be disbursed by credit to the Borrower's savings account within 3 working days of execution of this Agreement.

3. INTEREST
The Loan shall carry interest at the rate of 21% per annum, calculated on the reducing balance. The Lender may revise the rate of interest at its sole discretion from time to time, and such revised rate shall be binding on the Borrower from the date of the Lender's notice.

4. REPAYMENT
The Borrower shall repay the Loan together with interest in 36 equated monthly instalments of Rs. 18,838/- each. The first EMI shall be paid on 5th April, 2026 and subsequent EMIs on the Due Date of every month.

5. PREPAYMENT
The Borrower may prepay the entire outstanding Loan after payment of the first 12 EMIs, subject to a prepayment charge of 4% of the principal amount outstanding on the date of prepayment.

6. LATE PAYMENT CHARGES
If any EMI is not paid on its Due Date, the Borrower shall pay penal interest at the rate of 3% per month on the overdue amount from the Due Date until the date of actual payment.

7. EVENTS OF DEFAULT
Each of the following shall be an event of default: (a) non-payment of any EMI within 30 days of its Due Date; (b) any representation made by the Borrower proving to be false; (c) the Borrower becoming insolvent. On the occurrence of an event of default, the entire outstanding Loan shall become immediately due and payable.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Lender, and the seat of arbitration shall be Chennai.

9. NOTICES
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by registered post or email to the addresses stated above.
