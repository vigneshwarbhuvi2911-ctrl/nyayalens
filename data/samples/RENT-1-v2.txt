RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is executed at Chennai on 25th May 2026 between Mr. S. Ramasamy, aged 64 years, residing at 7, Second Street, Kotturpuram, Chennai 600085 (hereinafter called the "Lessor") and Ms. Divya Nair, aged 29 years, employed with a software company, permanent address 18, MG Road, Thrissur, Kerala (hereinafter called the "Lessee").

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the residential flat bearing No. 2A, Sai Residency, 10, Third Main Road, Gandhi Nagar, Adyar, Chennai 600020 (the "Premises"), and the Lessee has requested the Lessor to let out the Premises for residential use.

1. PERIOD
The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 months commencing from 1st June 2026. The Lessee shall not terminate the tenancy during the first 11 months, which shall be the lock-in period.

2. RENT
The Lessee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty Five Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of every month by bank transfer to the Lessor's account.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Lessee has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 3,00,000/- (Rupees Three Lakh only). The deposit shall be refunded after the Lessee vacates the Premises, after deducting unpaid rent and such other charges as the Lessor may determine.

4. RENEWAL AND ENHANCEMENT
The tenancy may be renewed by mutual consent, in which case the rent shall be enhanced by 5% over the last paid rent.

5. MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES
The Lessee shall pay the electricity and water charges as per actual consumption and the monthly apartment maintenance charges. Major repairs and structural repairs shall be the responsibility of the Lessor.

6. TERMINATION
The Lessor may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving 15 days' notice in writing to the Lessee.

7. USE OF PREMISES
The Lessee shall use the Premises only for residential purposes and shall not sub-let the Premises.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement in the presence of the witnesses below.
