LEAVE AND LICENSE / LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made at Bengaluru on 1 July 2026 by and between Mr. Harish Gowda, residing at 55, 8th Cross, Malleswaram, Bengaluru 560003 (the "Lessor"), and Mr. Karthik Iyer, residing at present at 12, Ring Road, Pune 411001 (the "Lessee").

1. TERM AND LOCK-IN
The lease shall be for a term of 36 months from 1 July 2026. The Lessee agrees to a lock-in period of 24 months, during which the Lessee shall not terminate this lease for any reason. If the Lessee vacates during the lock-in period, the Lessee shall pay rent for the entire remaining lock-in period.

2. MONTHLY RENT
The Lessee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 40,000/- in advance on or before the 1st of each month.

3. DEPOSIT
The Lessee shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 4,00,000/- (Rupees Four Lakh only), which shall be returned at the end of the lease after deducting all dues as determined by the Lessor.

4. ESCALATION
The monthly rent shall stand increased by 15% at the end of every 12 months.

5. TERMINATION
The Lessor may terminate this lease at any time by giving 30 days' notice to the Lessee. After the lock-in period, the Lessee may terminate this lease by giving 3 months' notice in writing to the Lessor.

6. USE
The premises shall be used only for the residence of the Lessee and his family.

7. JURISDICTION
Courts at Bengaluru alone shall have jurisdiction in respect of any dispute under this Agreement.

Signed by both parties on the date first written above.
