BUSINESS LOAN AGREEMENT

This Agreement dated 20 August 2025 is entered into between Vaigai Capital Private Limited, having its principal office at 8, Town Hall Road, Madurai 625001 (hereinafter the "Lender"), and Sri Lakshmi Traders, a proprietorship concern represented by its proprietor Mr. R. Ganesh, having its place of business at 112, East Masi Street, Madurai 625001 (hereinafter the "Borrower").

1. AMOUNT OF LOAN
The Lender has sanctioned a term loan of Rs. 12,00,000/- (Rupees Twelve Lakh only) to the Borrower for working capital requirements of its business.

2. INTEREST RATE
Interest shall be charged at a fixed rate of 16.5% per annum on the daily outstanding balance.

3. TENURE AND INSTALMENTS
The loan is repayable in 24 monthly instalments of Rs. 59,043/- each, the first of which shall fall due on 20 September 2025.

4. OVERDUE INTEREST
On any amount not paid on its due date, overdue interest at 2.5% per month shall be payable over and above the contracted rate, until the overdue amount is paid in full.

5. RIGHT OF SET-OFF AND LIEN
The Lender shall have a lien on all deposits and accounts of the Borrower and its proprietor with the Lender, and may set off any amount due under this Agreement against such deposits without notice to the Borrower.

6. GUARANTEE
The loan is guaranteed by Mrs. G. Lakshmi under a separate deed of guarantee of even date.

7. DEFAULT AND RECALL
If the Borrower defaults in payment of any two instalments, the Lender may recall the entire loan and take such action as it deems fit for recovery.

8. ARBITRATION
Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and the venue of arbitration shall be Madurai.

The Borrower confirms that the terms of this Agreement were read over and explained in Tamil and were understood.
