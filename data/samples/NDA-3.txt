NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made at Pune on 10th September 2025 between Saffron Mobility Private Limited, having its registered office at 5, Baner Road, Pune 411045 (the "Disclosing Party"), and Quill Design Studio, a partnership firm having its office at 19, FC Road, Pune 411004 (the "Receiving Party").

1. The Disclosing Party intends to share product drawings, prototype designs and supplier information with the Receiving Party for the purpose of designing the user interface of an electric scooter dashboard.

2. The Receiving Party shall hold all such information in strict confidence, shall use it only for the said purpose, and shall not copy or disclose it to any person without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

3. The obligations of confidence shall continue for a period of 2 years from the date of this Agreement.

4. For 12 months from the date of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall not solicit or hire any employee of the Disclosing Party with whom it worked on the project.

5. The aggregate liability of the Receiving Party for any breach of this Agreement shall be limited to the fees paid to it under the design engagement.

6. This Agreement shall be governed by Indian law, and courts at Pune shall have jurisdiction.

7. Any notice under this Agreement shall be given by email to the persons named in the engagement letter.

Executed by the authorised representatives of both parties.
